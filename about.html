About

I’m Fabian Hansson Vallgren, an aspiring game developer working in Unity and C#.

What pulls me in most is the systems under a game: an AI opponent that learns a card game by playing millions of rounds against itself, a pathfinding algorithm that finds the shortest route across Europe, or a save system that keeps a whole world in order. My biggest project, Skit Gubbe, started as my final school project, and I’m still working on it.

Outside of games, I’ve worked professionally with Python, TypeScript and HTML.
